# Customer Segmentation K-Means <br>

**Problem Statement**<br>
Malls and retail businesses face the ongoing challenge of understanding their customer base to develop targeted marketing strategies. To address this, we aim to build a customer segmentation system that categorizes customers based on their behavior, purchasing trends, and demographic data. This system will provide actionable insights to improve marketing efforts, enhance customer satisfaction, and increase profitability by tailoring promotional campaigns to specific customer segments.<br>

**Approach**<br>
The approach involves the following steps:<br>

**1. Data Collection and Preprocessing:** <br>
. Gather customer data, including demographics, purchase history, spending patterns, and frequency of visits.
. Clean the data to remove inconsistencies, handle missing values, and normalize numerical fields for analysis.<br>

**2. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** <br>
. Visualize the data to identify patterns, correlations, and outliers.<br>
. Assess key metrics such as customer lifetime value (CLV), average transaction size, and frequency of purchases.<br>

**3. Clustering Technique:** <br>
. Apply unsupervised machine learning, specifically K-Means clustering, to segment customers based on behavior and trends.<br>
. Use features such as Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM) metrics or other relevant attributes for clustering.<br>
. Determine the optimal number of clusters using techniques like the Elbow Method or Silhouette Score.<br>

**4. Validation and Profiling:** <br>
. Validate the segments by analyzing the characteristics of each cluster.<br>
. Profile each customer segment to provide meaningful business insights, such as identifying high-value customers or discount-driven shoppers.<br>

**5. Recommendations for Marketing Strategies:** <br>
. Develop targeted strategies for each segment, including personalized offers, loyalty programs, and promotional campaigns.<br>

**Analysis** <br>

**1. Data Analysis:** <br>
. Segment customers into distinct groups based on trends and patterns.<br>
. Visualize clusters to understand customer groupings and behaviors. <br>

**2. Insights Derived:** <br>
. Identify trends such as peak shopping times, popular product categories, and spending behavior.<br>
. Highlight high-value segments for focused marketing and areas with untapped potential.<br>

**3. Model Evaluation:** <br>
. Evaluate the clustering results using metrics like inertia, silhouette score, and cluster homogeneity.<br>
. Refine clusters by testing different feature combinations or algorithms (e.g., hierarchical clustering or DBSCAN).<br>

**Summary and Conclusion** <br>
By implementing a customer segmentation system, we effectively categorize customers into meaningful groups that align with their purchasing behavior and preferences. This segmentation enables malls to:<br>

. Optimize marketing campaigns by tailoring them to specific customer needs.<br>
. Increase customer engagement through personalized promotions and improved satisfaction.<br>
. Enhance profitability by focusing efforts on high-value customer segments.<br>
The proposed system not only improves marketing efficiency but also helps in long-term customer retention, ultimately leading to a more sustainable and customer-centric business strategy for malls.<br>
